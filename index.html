Controls & combat notes

Use the left stick to move; push to its edge to sprint. Drag the world to look. Tap Attack to strike and hold Jump for a higher leap. Dash and Powers appear as you earn them; open Powers, then tap an ability to use it. Vehicle buttons appear only nearby. While driving, use the stick to steer, hold Brake to slide, and tap Exit vehicle to get out.

Red rings warn of a strike. Move out, jump, or dash — dash just before it lands for a perfect dodge, or land your own blow in that moment to parry. A gunner's red line locks before he fires: sidestep, dash through the shot, or cut it back at him. Heavy blows and the whirl throw light enemies into the air; a strike in the air plunges onto everyone below. A car and a bike wait past every shrine. Traffic yields to people. Crashes transfer momentum and can overturn vehicles; use the vehicle button beside a stopped wreck to right it. Ramming throws enemies, but a punk survives one full-speed hit. The street has people on it too. Hurt a citizen and it costs you health; kill one and every citizen near turns on you. Hooded runners may be thieves (loot at the hip) and a thief costs nothing. Clear each sector to open its gate and learn an ability. Shrines heal and save your progress.
