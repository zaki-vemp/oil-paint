Code is MIT. Original procedural scenery is CC0; the character and creature licences are listed individually below.

- **Seed-san model by VirtualCast, Inc.** The traveler and all five mirror rivals use this model from the [VRM Consortium samples](https://github.com/vrm-c/vrm-specification/tree/master/samples/Seed-san), under the [VRM Public License 1.0](https://vrm.dev/licenses/1.0/). Its embedded permissions allow everyone to use it, commercial use, modification and redistribution; credit is required. The model is unmodified on disk. In-game changes include outfit tints, hidden mechanical accessories, boots and a scarf. `characters/portrait.png` is a render of this model. The complete licence is bundled in `characters/VRM-PUBLIC-LICENSE-1.0.pdf`.
- **Authored AA street props, landmarks, blades, vehicles, rider, civilians, specialists and foe rigs (`public/street/`):** twenty-six original headless Blender 4.2 LTS assets created programmatically for Komorebi (code MIT, authored geometry and animations CC0) — mission props (`terminal`, `supply-crate`, `power-core`, `beacon`, `barricade`), Neo-Kyoto landmarks (`noodle-stall`, `echo-shrine`), hero blades and lantern (`cyber-katana`, `ren-odachi`, `spirit-lantern`), vehicles and seated rider (`coupe`, `sports`, `van`, `motorbike`, `scooter`, `rider`), rigged citizens and specialists (`resident`, `mechanic`, `courier`, `executive`, `vendor`), and bespoke enemy rigs (`foe-punk`, `foe-enforcer`, `foe-gunner`, `foe-blade`, `foe-mech`). Built from `tools/blender_aa_lib.py` and `tools/build_aa_*.py` (superseding the earlier `tools/prepare-neon-assets.py` review pass); see `street/SOURCE.md`.
- **Fox:** model by PixelMannen (CC0); rigging and animation by tomkranis (CC BY 4.0); glTF conversion by Asobo Studio and scurest (CC BY 4.0). Downloaded unchanged from the [Khronos glTF Sample Assets Fox](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox). Rendered here with cel shading and normalized scale. See `creatures/FOX-LICENSE.md`.
- **Enemy cast (punk, enforcer, gunner, razor ronin, siege mech):** Quaternius, [Toon Shooter Game Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/toonshootergamekit.html) and [Cyberpunk Game Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html), CC0 1.0. Unmodified files, renamed; spare weapon props are hidden in game and colours are repainted through the shared cel shader. Mirrors and file mapping are in `enemies/SOURCE.md`.
- **Earlier citizens and hooded runners (retired, files kept):** original [Human Basemeshes](https://opengameart.org/content/human-basemeshes) by **pistachio** (CC0 1.0) and [Base Human Models (Low Poly)](https://opengameart.org/content/base-human-models-low-poly) by **Clint Bellanger** ([CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)). Komorebi adds clothing materials, headgear and six animation clips. See `people/SOURCE.md` for the original downloads and reproducible conversion. Earlier mirrored Quaternius pedestrians are retained but no longer loaded.
- **Earlier car body (retired, files kept):** **Eclipse SRC by shotman_16**, from **Jreo’s [VitaVehicle](https://jreo.itch.io/rcp4)** ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). Converted from the original Godot scene, resized, merged by material role and repainted; see `vehicles/SOURCE.md`. Wheels, neon trim, bike geometry and handling are original Komorebi work (code MIT, original geometry CC0).
- **Fox, Demon and BlueDemon (retired, files kept):** no longer spawned.

See `creatures/QUATERNIUS-SOURCE.md`.
